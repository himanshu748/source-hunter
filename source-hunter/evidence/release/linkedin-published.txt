I built Source Hunter for the moment before you quote a number.

Highlight a claim in Chrome, then inspect its wording, dates and citation trail in a local workbench. Broken links and missing originals stay visible.

The demo checks how a WHO estimate rounds 2,325.5 million to 2.33 billion. It also checks the claim that the Great Wall is visible to the naked eye from the Moon against NASA’s explanation.

TinyResearch proposes the trail, and TinyFish Fetch checks the pages.

The repo includes five recorded investigations and a separate fresh NOAA test, with original results and later review labelled separately. Earliest-source findings remain unresolved.

You can try the recorded examples locally without an API key. New research needs your own TinyFish access and configured limits.
