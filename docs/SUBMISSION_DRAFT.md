## Product description

Source Hunter helps a reader inspect a statistic before quoting it. Highlight a line on a public page, open the Chrome extension and inspect the source trail, wording, dates and unresolved questions.

The saved demo covers five real claims across public health, cybersecurity, astronomy and historical writing. The WHO food estimate has a supported rounding explanation. NASA contradicts a historical Great Wall Moon-visibility assertion; the selected article's skeptical stance stays separate from the claim it quotes.

TinyResearch proposes an investigation. TinyFish Fetch checks exact pages for wording and observed links. The workbench separates those observations from independently located comparisons, proposed attribution roles and failed retrievals. A matching sentence does not establish truth, primary authorship or earliest origin.
